Every payment does three jobs

Delivered by voice note on WhatsApp in Hindi, English or Hinglish. No new app to learn.

Job 1Route

Where to sell

Reads past sales by market, day and product. Says where to sell next and how many rivals are there.
“Kal Tuesday market jao. 4 aur pot sellers.”
Job 2Wallet

Fixed weekly salary

Holds back 10–30% and pays a fixed salary each Sunday. Lean-week cover tops up if it runs low.
“Kharab hafta mein bhi Sunday ko ₹1,500.”
Job 3Proof

A record lenders trust

One-tap rating from a real payer only. Ratings and weekly income build the Pakki Kamai Passbook.
“12 steady weeks. Lender dekh sakta hai.”

What exists, and what it misses

We found no product that combines market advice, a savings-first weekly salary and payment-verified ratings.

What existsWhat it doesWhat it does not do
Credit apps, PM SVANidhi loansLend moneyCreate debt. No income smoothing or market advice.
UPI sound-box appsConfirm payments, daily ledgerNo salary, advice or verified rating.
Savings appsHelp saveNot built around a seller’s weekly cycle.
Review sites and mapsShow reviewsNot tied to a real payment, so they can be faked.
EWA fintechs (Refyne, Wagely)Advance earned wagesNeed an employer with a payroll system.
Pakki KamaiRoute, wallet and proofNo debt. Free for the worker.
“EWA needs an employer with a payroll. Our user has none. We create a payroll where none exists, from the UPI payments the seller already receives.”
